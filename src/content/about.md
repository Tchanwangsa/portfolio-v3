I'm a recent computer science graduate originally from Bangkok, Thailand. I spend most of my time building products and exploring agentic engineering.

Early on, I picked up coding as a self-taught developer, learning Python and JavaScript by building an HTML Canvas football game and a Python text RPG.

At 19, I interned at [Boonrawd Brewery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boon_Rawd_Brewery) for a month, where I mocked up an [event seating system](https://tchanwangsa.github.io/EventManager/index.html) to modernise the UI/UX of their legacy tool.

I also joined the [Data Science Student Society](https://www.dscubed.org.au/) as an IT Officer, revamping the club's website.

I later moved up to IT Director and eventually Products Director, where I started [Connect3](https://connect3.app/), a centralised platform for campus discovery.

After shipping products and watching AI agents take off, I saw an opportunity to build a context layer connecting university coursework to agents.

That became [Project Oculus](https://github.com/oculus-app/oculus), an agentic workspace I'm currently building so students can interact with their coursework in ways that weren't possible before.
